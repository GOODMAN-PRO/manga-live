# 湯あがり — YUAGARI
### *(fresh out of the bath)*

*Yuagari* is the word for the state of a person who has just left hot water:
flushed, loose, clean, and completely undefended. That is the entire series.

---

## Logline

The coldest girl in class secretly runs her dead grandfather's dying bathhouse
alone every night; the loudest boy in class is secretly raising two children on
no sleep — and after closing, in a building whose only job is to make tired
people warm, they become the first person who ever saw the other one tired.

---

## The two leads

### Shirakawa Nagi (白川 凪) — 17, second year

| | |
|---|---|
| **Public self** | First on every ranking. Answers in four words. Declines everything. Class nickname: **「無風」 — *Mufū*, "windless."** They think it's clever. It's her name with the warmth taken out. |
| **Hidden self** | Sole operator of **Kotobuki-yu (寿湯)** — "longevity bath" — since her grandfather died fourteen months ago. Lights the boiler at 5:40, opens at 6, closes at 11:30, does homework at the counter. Nobody at school knows the building exists. |
| **Wound** | Her grandfather raised her; her parents are alive, remarried, and elsewhere. She has never once said out loud that she misses him. Keeping the boiler lit is not sentiment — it is the argument she is having with the fact that he is gone. |
| **Want** | Keep Kotobuki-yu open. |
| **Need** | To be allowed to be tired. To let something end without that meaning she failed it. |
| **Comedy register** | Devastating flat deadpan. She is not joking. It is funnier that she is not joking. |
| **Tell** | Her hands. Split knuckles, chemical-red, cracked from hot water — she keeps her blazer sleeves down over them at school, in every season. |

**Visual lock (art team — token source)**
- **Hair:** waist-length, dead straight, blue-black → *greyscale: solid black with one crisp white highlight band.* Centre part. School: loose or low ponytail. Night: twisted up in a plastic clip, damp strands stuck at the nape.
- **Eyes:** narrow, outer corners slightly downturned, dark irises, long lashes, default half-lidded. They almost never widen — when they do, it owns the whole panel.
- **Build:** 168cm, thin, very straight posture, long neck, visible collarbones.
- **School outfit:** navy blazer, grey pleated skirt, white blouse buttoned to the throat, navy ribbon, black knee socks, **sleeves always down.**
- **Night outfit:** faded navy samue trousers, white tee, short indigo happi with 寿湯 on the back, sleeves shoved past the elbow, rolled towel around the neck, blue rubber sandals — her grandfather's, a size too big.
- **Props:** plastic hair clip, chipped tile brush, a wrench she hits the boiler gauge with.

### Nomura Itsuki (野村 樹) — 17, second year

| | |
|---|---|
| **Public self** | The reason class is bearable. Late every day, never absent, does bits, takes the punishment, makes the quiet kids laugh. Everyone calls him **Nomu**. |
| **Hidden self** | Eldest of three. Mother works night shifts at a care home; father left four years ago. He cooks, does laundry, runs baths, checks homework, and handles fevers at 2am. He has told no one, because the moment he's pitied he stops being the funny one, and being the funny one is the one thing he's good at. |
| **Wound** | Nobody has ever asked him a question he had to answer honestly. He is enormously loved and completely unknown, and he arranged that himself. |
| **Want** | For everyone to be fine, indefinitely, without noticing what it costs. |
| **Need** | To be taken care of once, and not deflect it. |
| **Comedy register** | Full-body, escalating, self-sacrificing. The bits are real; the timing of the bits is armour. |
| **Tell** | His eyes. Default is laughing crescents with big catchlights. When it drops, the eyes go flat and the catchlights shrink to pinpricks — and it happens for exactly one panel before he rebuilds the face. |

**Visual lock (art team — token source)**
- **Hair:** messy, mid-length, dark brown → *greyscale: mid-grey tone, darker at the roots, white highlight.* Fringe to the eyebrows, permanent cowlick at the crown.
- **Eyes:** large, round, light irises → *greyscale: light grey, oversized catchlights.* Thick lower lash line.
- **Build:** 175cm, lean, slightly hunched, loose-limbed, hands in pockets.
- **School outfit:** uniform worn wrong — blazer off or over one shoulder, tie loose or pocketed, sleeves shoved up, **green-trimmed school jersey** over the shirt, scuffed sneakers.
- **Night outfit:** same jersey, sweatpants, canvas tote of groceries.
- **Props:** a girl's hair tie on his left wrist (Mio's), a plaster on one finger (cooking), the jersey. Always at least two of the three.

## Setting

**Kotobuki-yu (寿湯)** — a 1961 sentō on the **Sakuragawa shotengai**, a shopping
street two-thirds shuttered. Tiled Mt-Fuji mural, chipped in one corner. Rows of
yellow plastic baskets. A fish-shaped clock that runs four minutes fast. A
massage chair that eats 100 yen and does nothing. A goods shelf with dusty stock
from before. A pair of worn sandals by the counter that Nagi has not moved.

The city has plans for the street. They arrive as envelopes, not as villains.

Modern-day Japan. Public world: an ordinary prefectural high school, harsh and
bright. Private world: 11:31pm onward, steam, tile, one warm lamp, and the sound
of water on stone.

## Tone rules

1. **Max ~14 words per bubble.** Split, or cut.
2. **If a line announces its own sadness, it is deleted.** Nobody says "I've been so lonely." They say "Then another night."
3. **Silence is the default, not the exception.** A page may end on a face and nothing else. It should, often.
4. **Chibi is a release valve, never a substitute.** Never inside a sincere beat — only immediately before or after one.
5. **No villains.** The adults are tired, not cruel. The city is slow, not evil.
6. **No escalation into disease, death, or triangles.** The stakes are a boiler, a bill, and two people's exhaustion. That is enough.
7. **Comedy comes from character, not from bits.** Itsuki's bits are funny because we know what they're covering.
8. **Warmth accumulates through chores.** Scrubbing, folding, carrying, boiling. Never through speeches.
9. **The two leads never explain themselves to each other in full.** Information leaks. One sentence per chapter, maximum.

## 14-chapter arc map

| # | Chapter | Emotional beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The water's still hot.** Midnight rain, a shut street, one lit building. Both masks come off in a single look. | Discovery. Being seen without permission. |
| 2 | He comes back — at opening hours, with two kids, as a paying customer. She hates how much it stings. | The gap between *customer* and *let in after closing*. |
| 3 | He stays past 11:30 and picks up a brush without being asked. Forty minutes of scrubbing, nine words spoken. | Warmth as labour. The first night that belongs to them. |
| 4 | Gonda nearly follows him. A near-miss at school; both leads lie badly and then well. | Comedy peak one. The secret becomes something they hold *together*. |
| 5 | Sōta decides Nagi is his. Calls her something mortifying in front of Fusae. She is startlingly good with him and it frightens her. | Warm + comedy. She is capable of a life she has not allowed herself. |
| 6 | The sandals by the counter. He asks one question. She gives one sentence: *"He wore these."* | First crack. Grief admitted by object, not by speech. |
| 7 | The envelope gets opened. Redevelopment consultation, and a boiler certificate she can't afford. She hides both; he finds both. | Tension. The clock starts — quietly, on paper. |
| 8 | **MID-SERIES GUT-PUNCH.** The boiler fails. Three nights without sleep, and she goes down on the wet tile. He carries her to the rest area, and asks the question nobody has asked in fourteen months: *"Are you tired?"* She says the smaller, worse thing — *"If I stop, it's just a building"* — and cries in a way she despises. | The wound, finally seen. This is the chapter the series exists for. |
| 9 | Aftermath. They are unbearable at school. A confession misfires into a fight in a stairwell. | Intimacy has a cost. Aya becomes a real friend by refusing to be managed. |
| 10 | His turn. Mio says it out loud: he isn't applying anywhere. He laughs it off. Nagi doesn't let him. She is the first person ever furious *on his behalf.* | The second wound. Being cared for, and not deflecting it. |
| 11 | The street fights back — a one-night festival to keep Kotobuki-yu open. Ban, Fusae, the whole shotengai, and a hundred and forty people in a building for sixty. | Comedy peak two, warmth peak. Community as love. |
| 12 | It isn't enough, and it was never going to be. The offer to sell. Nagi has to choose between the building and her own life. | The real question: what is she actually keeping alive? |
| 13 | **Closing night.** The last bath. The whole street comes. She lets the water go cold on purpose, and in the empty bath, says the thing she has never said — about her grandfather, and then about him. | Grief finally permitted. The confession lands *in* the loss, not after it. |
| 14 | **FINALE.** Spring. Kotobuki-yu is something else now. Nagi sleeps eight hours for the first time in two years. Itsuki's siblings have someone else in the house. Final image echoes page one: a shut street, one lit thing, a door held open — and this time two people inside it. | Not "they got together." *They were known.* The relationship isn't the climax; being seen was. |

**Mid-series gut-punch:** Ch.8 — *"Are you tired?"*
**Finale:** Ch.14 — the door held open.

## Series motifs

- **Water temperature** as emotional register: scalding, right, gone cold.
- **Hands** — hers cracked and hidden; his bandaged and busy. They are the first thing each notices about the other, and neither says so until Ch.13.
- **Sound as recognition** — she knows his footsteps on wet stone before she turns around. He knows the fish clock's tick.
- **The noren** going up and coming down. Every chapter opens or closes on it.
