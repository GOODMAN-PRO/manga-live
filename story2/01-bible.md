# 余熱 — YONETSU
### *(the heat that stays)*

*Yonetsu* is residual heat: what's left warm in a thing after the fire has gone
out. It is also what the tower cannot take.

---

## Logline

For a year, the two most feared divers in Tōka Academy's tower have trusted each
other absolutely and never seen each other's faces — and outside it they're the
untouchable top-ranked prodigy and the magicless boy the class laughs at. When
they find out, they get everything they never had in daylight: banter, lunch,
someone who knows. The tower charges one thread of feeling per layer cleared, and
they are climbing anyway.

## The tower, and the cost of climbing

**Tōka Academy** trains divers. At its centre is the **Tower** — entered by dive,
climbed by **layer**. Your body in there is a generated **figure**; your name in
there is a **handle** you don't choose, the front line coins it for you. Identity
is sealed by regulation, so the rankings can't be bought or bullied. This is why
two people can be inseparable for a year and strangers at breakfast.

**The toll.** Clearing a layer costs one **thread** — a capacity, taken from the
outside in. Nobody chooses which. The order is roughly known:

> taste → smell → temperature on the skin → pain → touch → being startled →
> finding things funny → wanting things → the rest

It does not come back. Your total is your **count**, and counts are semi-public.
At the exit gate there is a **calibration**: a scent card, a metal plate at two
temperatures, a pin, a sugar strip, and a technician who writes a number down.

Kou is on **nine**. Kanade is on **four**. Second-year average is one.
Nobody who has gone past twenty has ever come back down the stairs.

**The return** — a rumoured layer where the tower gives back. Unverified.
Front-liners talk about it the way sailors talk about islands.

## Tone rules

1. **They talk.** Deflection, banter, technical chatter, arguing about nothing. Density is the point; silence is a contrast, deployed once or twice a chapter and never as a default.
2. **Nobody names a feeling until the series has earned it.** They name *tactics*, *food*, *handles*, *rules*, and each other's failings.
3. **The register switch is the house style.** Deformed panels fire several times a chapter, mid-scene, including inside serious rooms. The abruptness is the joke and the armour.
4. **Comedy protects sincerity; sincerity redeems comedy.** A gag page must be followed within two pages by something true.
5. **The cost is stated in numbers, never in speeches.** Nobody says "it's killing me." They say "nine."
6. **No villains.** The institution is tired. The class is careless, not evil. The tower has no opinion.
7. **Discovery is a beginning.** Everything good in this series happens *after* they know.
8. **Physical stakes are real.** What happens in the tower comes out with you. Wounds, exhaustion, threads.
9. **She escalates, he doesn't.** That gap generates most of the chapter's comedy and all of its tenderness.

| # | Chapter | Emotional beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blank Pin.** Layer 31 with NULL and KETTLE; then daylight, where they've spoken eleven sentences in two years. He shields her; she bandages his arm with her own knot. Next morning she sees that knot in her classroom. | Discovery's trigger. The two halves of two people, in one chapter. |
| 2 | She says nothing for four days and then says it in the worst possible place. He doesn't deny it. **Both of them are in breach; each is now the other's weapon; neither picks it up.** | Discovery as a beginning. Custody, not leverage. |

| 9 | The ban is enforced. She's pulled off the front line, and NULL goes up alone for the first time in a year. | Separation. What each is without the other. |

## Motifs

- **Numbers as feeling.** Counts, layers, temperatures, times. Nobody says *I'm scared*; they say a figure.
- **Bare hands vs gloves.** Hers. The tell, the theme, and the last image of the series.
- **The blank pin.** His. Everyone reads it as nothing. It is the reason he is allowed nowhere and goes everywhere.
- **Food.** She eats gloriously; he cannot taste. It's a running gag for four chapters and then it isn't.
- **The cloth-wrapped bundle.** Nobody asks. Ch.1 doesn't answer.
